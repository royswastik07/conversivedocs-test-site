Features

Content version: 2.0 — If you see this text in the agent's answers, the URL Update re-crawl is working correctly.

Smart Search

Full-text and semantic search across all your documents. Now with filters by author, date, and space — so teams find exactly what they need in seconds.

Real-time Collaboration

Multiple teammates can edit documents simultaneously. Version history now goes back 365 days, up from 30 days in the previous release.

Integrations

ConversiveDocs connects with Slack, Jira, GitHub, Figma, and now Notion. Documents are automatically linked to related issues and pull requests.

AI-Powered Summaries

Every document gets an AI-generated summary. New in v2.0: summaries are now available in 12 languages including Spanish, French, German, and Japanese.

Access Control

Set permissions at the workspace, space, or page level. New in v2.0: time-limited guest access — external links now expire after 7, 30, or 90 days.

Analytics Dashboard

New in v2.0: see which documents are most viewed, which are never read, and who your most active contributors are — all in one dashboard.

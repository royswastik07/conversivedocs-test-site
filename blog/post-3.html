5 Signs Your Knowledge Base Needs a Refresh

October 6, 2026 · By the ConversiveDocs Team · 4 min read

A knowledge base that was great six months ago can quietly become a liability. Here are five signals that it's time to refresh yours.

1. Support tickets ask questions your docs already answer

If your support team keeps answering the same question, the docs either don't exist or are impossible to find. Search your ticket backlog for the top 10 recurring questions and check whether each one has a clear, findable answer in the knowledge base.

2. Pages haven't been viewed in 90 days

An unread page is either irrelevant or buried. Pull a view-count report and archive anything with zero views in the last quarter. Less is more — a leaner knowledge base is easier to search and easier to trust.

3. Teammates say "just ask me" instead of linking a doc

When people default to Slack DMs over sending a link, they've lost confidence that the docs are accurate. The fix is visible: run a content audit, update stale pages publicly, and tell the team you did it.

4. Onboarding new hires takes longer than it used to

New hire ramp time is a direct measure of knowledge base health. If it's creeping up, the docs haven't kept pace with how the product or process actually works today.

5. Nobody knows who owns which page

Orphaned pages go stale fast. Every page should have an owner — ideally the person who uses that feature most, not the person who wrote the original doc years ago. ConversiveDocs shows the owner on every page and sends a quarterly nudge to review their pages.

Getting Started

This guide walks you through creating your first workspace and inviting your team.

Step 1: Create a workspace

Go to app.ConversiveDocs.example.com/signup and enter your work email. A verification link will be sent. Click it to activate your account.

Step 2: Set up your first space

A space is a top-level container for related documents. Create one for each team, product, or project. Click + New Space in the sidebar and give it a name.

Step 3: Import existing content

ConversiveDocs can import from Notion, Confluence, Google Docs, and plain Markdown files. Go to Settings → Import and follow the wizard.

Step 4: Invite your team

Go to Settings → Members and enter email addresses. Invited users receive an email to join your workspace. You can set roles per space: viewer, editor, or admin.

Step 5: Connect an integration

Go to Settings → Integrations and connect Slack or Jira. Once connected, ConversiveDocs will post a summary whenever a document is published in a watched space.
